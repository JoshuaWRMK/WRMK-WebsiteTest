WRMK Lawyers' Dargaville office has proudly served the wider Kaipara community since 2008.

WRMK Lawyers' Dargaville office offers a full range of legal services including property and conveyancing, employment law, business/commercial law, advice on rural matters, wills and estates, elder law, trusts and asset planning, and dispute resolution. Dargaville is a hub for many of our farming and rural clients.

Led by Directors Alice Chapman and Rebecca Merry, our team of lawyers includes Dargaville locals Lina Khan, Natalie Robinson, Dion Metcalf, and Legal Executive Tania McMinn.

WRMK Lawyers is delighted to be involved in the local community, including sponsoring the Dargaville Primary School Nature Playground.

Our legal team moved into new premises at 118 Victoria Street (across the road from the original office) on 2 October 2020.
