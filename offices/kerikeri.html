LEGAL SUPPORT FOR ALL OF THE FAR NORTH

WRMK's Kerikeri office offers a full range of legal services including property and conveyancing, wills and estates, elder law, trusts and asset planning, business/commercial law, employment law, advice on rural matters and dispute resolution.

Led by Director Chloe Davenport, our Kerikeri legal team includes local Associates Rachel Ivess and Surrey Coombes, Senior Lawyer Amelia Roberts, Lawyer Georgia McDonald and Legal Executives Sarah O'Brien and Sarah Kayll.

WRMK Lawyers is delighted to be involved in the local community, and is a proud sponsor of the Kerikeri Cricket Club.

WRMK Lawyers' Kerikeri office opened in 2016.

Address: Street level, John Butler Centre, 60 Kerikeri Road, Kerikeri 0230
Phone: 09 401 6354
Office hours: 9am-4.30pm Monday - Friday or by appointment
Car parking: Free parking is available on Kerikeri Road and nearby streets.
