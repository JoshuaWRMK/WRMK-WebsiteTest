WRMK Lawyers' head office is based in Whangarei.

Founded in the 1930s as Webb Ross & Co, the firm merged with Urlich McNab Kilpatrick in 2012 to become WRMK Lawyers, the largest law firm in Whangarei.

WRMK's Whangarei office offers a full range of legal services including property and conveyancing, wills and estates, elder law, trusts and asset planning, business/commercial law, employment law, advice on rural matters, dispute resolution and criminal law.

WRMK Lawyers is delighted to be involved in the local community, and is a proud supporter of the Northland Cancer Society, Whangarei Hundertwasser Arts Centre, and Camera Obscura Whangarei.
