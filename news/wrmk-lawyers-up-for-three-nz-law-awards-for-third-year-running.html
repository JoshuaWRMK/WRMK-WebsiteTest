WRMK Lawyers up for three NZ Law Awards for third year runningOriginal content

30 August 2021Employment

WRMK NAMED 2021 NZ LAW AWARDS EXCELLENCE AWARDEE

WRMK Lawyers has been named an Excellence Awardee for three New Zealand Law Awards again in 2021.

Northland’s largest law firm, WRMK Lawyers is in the running to be named Mid-size Law Firm of the Year, Regional/Suburban Law Firm of the Year and Employer of Choice (less than 50 employees). Winners for each category will be announced at a ceremony on 18 November 2021.

WRMK Lawyers Managing Director Rebecca Merry says, “Our firm culture is built around flexibility, enabled by technology, and working together as a team for our clients. These foundations are crucial for allowing us to continue to deliver excellent service, despite the trying conditions brought about by COVID-19. We are delighted to see our Northland firm recognised in three award categories for the third year in a row.”

WRMK Lawyers is the only law firm in the North to be nominated for an award.

WRMK won the Regional/Suburban Law Firm of the Year award in both 2019 and 2020.

The NZ Law Awards are the leading independent awards for the New Zealand legal profession, and judge work completed and services provided between 1 April 2020 and 31 March 2021.
