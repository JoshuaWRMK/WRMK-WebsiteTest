WRMK Lawyers celebrates five new leadersOriginal content

17 April 2025Property

WRMK Lawyers is delighted to announce the promotion of a new Special Counsel and four Senior Lawyers, effective from April 2025.

The firm congratulates David Adams on his promotion to Special Counsel. The Special Counsel designation recognises David’s specialised knowledge, expertise and experience and is used only for a very select group of senior lawyers.

WRMK Lawyers’ Managing Director Rebecca Merry says “David’s expertise in relationship property and trusts and estates litigation is recognised across the legal community, and we are delighted to celebrate this with his promotion to Special Counsel. David is known for his sharp intellect, very thorough knowledge of the law, and strong advocacy, and his new title reflects this.”

Congratulations are also deserved for Amelia Roberts, Jonathon Riley, Keegan Jones, and Maddie Doherty who have been promoted to Senior Lawyer.

Merry says, “We are delighted to celebrate these promotions, which reflect the high calibre of our lawyers. They each demonstrate exceptional client service, sound legal expertise, and leadership, and we are proud to recognise and congratulate them on their achievements.”

NEW SPECIAL COUNSEL

DAVID ADAMS, DISPUTE RESOLUTION (WHANGAREI)

David is a specialist in relationship property and contentious trust and estate matters within WRMK’s Dispute Resolution team, with extensive experience in both the Family Court and High Court. He is a member of AMINZ, an affiliate of STEP, a founding trustee of Tai Tokerau Family Law Trust, and is frequently involved in complex disputes involving family businesses, estates, and high-value assets.

NEW SENIOR LAWYERS

AMELIA ROBERTS, DISPUTE RESOLUTION (KERIKERI/WHANGAREI)

Amelia is a criminal law specialist in the Dispute Resolution team with over four years’ experience representing clients in the District Court and High Court. She began her legal career working in house at a local Council. Amelia started work in criminal law in 2020 as an employed barrister in Auckland, where she assisted on a broad range of criminal and regulatory prosecutions, before moving to Northland and joining WRMK Lawyers in 2024.

JONATHON RILEY, DISPUTE RESOLUTION (WHANGAREI)

Jonathon is part of the dispute resolution team, specialising in criminal law. He began his legal career with the Prosecution Service, and is now an experienced advocate in civil and criminal proceedings in the District Court and High Court, with a particular focus on defending land transport, animal welfare, and firearms-related prosecutions.

KEEGAN JONES, BUSINESS LAW (WHANGAREI)

Keegan is a member of the business team, specialising in commercial law, construction and business sale and purchases. He is a Law Society Auckland Branch Council member and, before entering private practice, he worked in the public sector as an advisor for Te Puni Kokiri (Ministry of Māori Development).

MADDIE DOHERTY, ESTATES AND LIFE PLANNING (WHANGAREI)

Maddie is a member of the Estates and Life Planning team, specialising in relationship property and family law. She has particular experience advising clients on separations—both contentious and non-contentious—and regularly prepares, and provides independent legal advice on, contracting out agreements to help clients plan for and protect their property interests in the context of their relationships. Maddie also works across estate planning and disputes, and assists with matters involving personal care and welfare, parenting, and family violence.
