About us

Northland's largest law firm, since the 1930s

We're based in four offices across Northland — Whangārei, Dargaville, Kerikeri and Warkworth — and we were the first New Zealand law firm to go fully digital. Today that's 11 directors, 26 lawyers, 14 legal executives and a wider support team covering everything from property and employment to wills, trusts and family disputes.

We care about our people as much as our clients — a good working environment for our team is part of how we look after you.

WRMK Lawyers is Northland's largest law firm. Based in four offices across the North, we are proud of our reputation of being there for our clients whenever they need us. We believe in the importance of communication and being accessible, and have a strong focus on innovation, performance and excellence. We were the first New Zealand law firm to successfully transition to a digital office.

Previously also known as Webb Ross McNab Kilpatrick, our history dates back to the 1930s. Today, we have 11 directors, 26 lawyers, 14 legal executives and an amazing team of support people serving our clients in the areas of commercial, property, rural, employment, wills and estates, trusts, asset/wealth protection, litigation/disputes and relationship and family property.

We believe that the wellbeing of our people is critical to the success of our firm, and are proud to provide an excellent working environment and support network to all of our team to ensure they are happy and healthy at work.
