Dispute resolutionOriginal content

Negotiation, mediation and litigation.

Pick the right tool to resolve your dispute

Not every dispute needs a courtroom. We handle everything from contract and business disputes to property, trust, employment and relationship property fights — and we start by working out whether negotiation, mediation, arbitration or court is actually the fastest, cheapest way to sort your particular problem.

We're based right across the road from the Whangarei High Court, and our team ranges from junior solicitors to senior counsel, so you get the right level of firepower for the size of the dispute — not more than you need.

LET OUR EXPERIENCED ADVOCATES RESOLVE YOUR DISPUTES

Disputes come in many different forms, so it’s important to choose the right tool for the job when it comes to resolving them. Whether through mediation, arbitration or going to court, our experienced litigation and dispute resolution team will find the best solution for you.

We deal with a wide range of disputes and litigation involving:

  • breach of contracts
  • business disputes
  • employment disputes
  • relationship property (including separation and divorce)
  • trust and estate disputes
  • debt collection, liquidation and insolvency
  • land and property
  • building and construction (including payment claims)
  • Worksafe investigations and prosecutions
  • resource management, and
  • maritime matters.

Enforcing contractual obligations and/or legal rights requires a well-articulated and reasoned approach. Our civil litigation specialists have the legal expertise and know-how to maximise leverage and tactical advantage in all areas of civil litigation.

Whether it is a complex contractual dispute, property dispute, or a straightforward debt recovery, our civil litigation team can provide practical, efficient and effective advice, achieving the best possible results via correspondence, negotiations, mediation and the District and/or High Courts.

Our court lawyers and trained mediators are experienced in mediation, arbitration and in taking cases in the District Court, Family Court, High Court, Environment Court, Court of Appeal, Supreme Court and the Māori Land Court. We can also represent you before specialist tribunals (such as the Building Disputes Tribunal) as needed.

Our broad team allows us to offer the appropriate level of representation for the matter at hand, from junior solicitors to senior counsel – no need to use a sledgehammer to crack a nut! We’re also conveniently located in modern premises immediately over the road from the Whangarei High Court.
