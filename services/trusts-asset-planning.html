Trusts: setting up, winding up, or keeping compliant

A trust can protect what you've built — from business risk, from relationship property claims, from an inheritance being lost to a future divorce — but only if it's set up properly and kept up to date. We help with all three stages: setting one up, running it day-to-day so it stays compliant as the law changes, and winding it up if it's no longer needed.

Not sure whether a trust is right for you? Give us a call and we'll talk through whether it actually fits your situation.

PROTECT YOUR WEALTH

Protecting the things you and your family have worked hard for is important. Trusts are an excellent tool for protecting your assets and ensuring they pass on to the right people. In some situations, they may also help you to manage your tax obligations. However, it’s important to set them up and administer them correctly to ensure you achieve what you set out to.

Our trusts and asset planning team are experts with many years of specialist experience. We can help you set up and administer your trust to best fit your unique situation, or wind up your existing trust if it is no longer fit for purpose.

If you’d like to find out more about whether a trust could be right for you, your family or your business, please give us a call.

I want to set up a trust....+

A trust is an excellent tool for managing your assets. A trust can

  • protect personal assets from business risk
  • be more tax efficient
  • protect assets from relationship property and Family Protection Act claims
  • ensure a child keeps their inheritance
  • protect assets for future generations
  • provide flexibility for your estate planning needs, and
  • manage assets for blended families.

If you’re thinking about succession planning, what happens to your assets when you pass away, or protecting assets for future generations, it’s a good time to think about whether a trust would help you meet your objectives.

I no longer need my trust....+

If your trust is no longer fit for purpose, or it has achieved its objective, it may no longer be needed. Our team can help you through the process of winding up the trust and redistributing any assets to the appropriate parties.

Trust administration...+

Trusts are not “one and done”. There are certain things that need to be done on a regular basis, or when laws change, to keep your trust current and compliant. Our trust administration team can help you keep on top of those tasks and ensure your trust is doing what it is meant to, in the way it is meant to.
