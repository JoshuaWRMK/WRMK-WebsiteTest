Property development & subdivisionsOriginal content

Consents, subdivision and unit titles.

Subdividing or developing? Get the plan right early.

Whether it's a lifestyle block, an industrial site or a residential development, we work with owners, farmers and developers from the first due-diligence check through to acquisition, subdivision and sell-down — including the local authority requirements that trip up projects that skip this step.

We also help with boundary changes, amalgamations, easements, land covenants, unit titles and Public Works Act matters.

CHOOSE THE RIGHT TEAM TO MAKE YOUR REALTY VISION A REALITY

Whether you’re looking to subdivide lifestyle, industrial or residential blocks, our team of legal experts can help with all aspects of your development.

We work with property owners, farmers and developers to understand and help realise your long-term goals. Our lawyers and legal executives advise clients from acquisition through to subdivision and sell down, helping to determine complex local authority requirements and recommending solutions.

We can help with:

  • due diligence
  • acquisitions
  • subdivisions
  • boundary changes
  • amalgamations
  • construction
  • sales
  • easements
  • land covenants
  • unit titles, and
  • the Public Works Act.

Make sure your project has a plan that’s well thought out. Work with us.
